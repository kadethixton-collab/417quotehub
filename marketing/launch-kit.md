# 417 Quote Hub — Free Traffic Launch Kit

Everything here is ready to copy and paste. Total time: about 30 minutes once, then about 10 minutes a week.

**Ground rules that keep the brand trusted:**
- Post as yourself and say you run 417 Quote Hub. Never pose as a "neighbor who used it."
- Read each group's rules first. Many local groups ban business posts except on certain days. Follow that.
- Lead with help, not links. Give the link when someone asks or when it truly answers their question.
- Never claim we do the concrete work. We match homeowners with independent local contractors.

---

## 1. Your 10-minute weekly routine

1. **Monday (5 min):** search your local Facebook groups for "concrete," "driveway," "patio," and "sunken." Reply to new posts using the templates in section 3.
2. **Wednesday (3 min):** share one guide or city page in a group that allows business posts (section 2).
3. **Friday (2 min):** check the Google Sheet. Did any unmatched territories get leads? Forward 1–2 to a contractor you're pitching.

## 4. Free directory listings (one-time, about 20 minutes)

These give you trusted links and help people find you. Some directories require a physical address or don't accept referral services. Skip those rather than bending the truth.

> **Google Business Profile:** not eligible. Google's rules don't allow lead-generation or referral businesses, so don't create one. Your Google traffic comes from the website itself.

| Directory | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Facebook Page** | Create a "417 Quote Hub" page (category: *Home Improvement Service* or *Referral Service*). Post your guides there too. |
| **Nextdoor Business Page** | Use a business page for promotion; personal-account promotion is restricted on Nextdoor. |
| **Bing Places for Business** | Service-area businesses can hide their address. |
| **Apple Business Connect** | Free; shows in Apple Maps & Siri. |
| **Alignable** | Free local small-business network; good for meeting contractors too. |
| **LinkedIn Company Page** | Helps credibility with contractors. |
